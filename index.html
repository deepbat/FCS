FCS Attendance - Rules and Operating Reference

This is the complete reference for attendance, UT, SL, OT and report calculations. If a rule is changed, this reference should be updated at the same time.

1. Purpose

  • FCS Attendance is used to enter daily timings from the gate register and maintain monthly attendance and overtime records.

2. Employees and Normal Timings

  • Staff: 9:00am to 5:45pm; 45-minute break; normal duty span 8 hours 45 minutes; not eligible for OT.
  • Driver: 9:00am to 5:45pm; 45-minute break; eligible for OT.
  • Gardener: 8:30am to 5:10pm; 40-minute break; normal duty span 8 hours 40 minutes; not eligible for OT.
  • Gateman: timing is flexible; normal duty target and break are configurable; eligible for OT. Current normal target is 525 minutes.

3. Overtime (OT)

  • OT is based on elapsed duty span compared with normal duty span.
  • For Driver and Gateman, up to 15 minutes above normal duty does not count as OT.
  • Therefore, for a normal 9:00am to 5:45pm day: 6:00pm = 0 OT; 6:01pm = 16 minutes OT; 7:01pm = 1 hour 16 minutes OT.
  • Break is not deducted when deciding OT.
  • Staff and Gardener always have 0 OT.

4. Sunday and Holiday Duty

  • Driver and Gateman working on Sunday or an entered holiday are treated as full-day OT.
  • Entire duty time is OT.
  • No break is deducted on Sunday/holiday duty.
  • Sunday is identified automatically.
  • Holidays are maintained under Admin > Holidays.
  • Sunday/holiday duty does not receive UT or SL.

5. Under Time (UT)

  • UT applies to OT-eligible employees when they are called early for office work.
  • Normal start for this rule is 9:00am.
  • For Driver and Gateman, IN before 8:40am is treated as an early call and UT is given.
  • UT = 9:00am minus actual IN time.
  • Example: 7:30am IN = 1 hour 30 minutes UT.
  • 8:40am or later = no UT.
  • A person merely arriving early at or after 8:40am does not receive UT.
  • Sunday and holiday duty never receives UT.
  • Gautam special rule: official duty start is 7:30am. Arrival before 7:30am is voluntary early arrival and gives no UT. From 7:30am up to 9:00am, UT is calculated against 9:00am.

6. Short Leave (SL)

  • SL is calculated from actual IN time against employee's applicable normal start time.
  • For a normal 9:00am start, IN at 9:20am gives 20 minutes SL.
  • For Gautam, the official start is 7:30am for the special duty arrangement, so early arrival before 7:30am does not create UT and arrival after 7:30am is treated according to applicable SL/attendance rules.
  • Sunday and holiday duty does not receive SL.
  • SL can be manually adjusted in Admin for exceptional situations, such as approved short leave during the day.

7. Varinder Pal - Split Shift

  • Evening duty: approximately 6:00pm to 1:00am.
  • Morning duty: approximately 6:00am to 7:00am.
  • Normal total duty is 8 hours.
  • Sleep period from approximately 1:00am to 6:00am is not counted as duty.
  • No break deduction.
  • Time is rounded to 30-minute intervals.
  • Both periods are stored in the background; normal register display can show only final OUT time.
  • Example: 6:00pm to 1:00am + 6:00am to 7:00am = 8 hours, 0 OT.
  • Example: 6:00pm to 1:00am + 6:00am to 8:00am = 9 hours, 1 hour OT.

8. Attendance Status

  • Staff: Present, First Half Leave, Second Half Leave, Full Day Leave.
  • Other employees: Present, Absent, Leave, Half Day, Holiday, Sunday.
  • Sunday and entered holidays are identified automatically.

9. Staff Auto-Suggestion

  • IN by 9:30am and OUT by 1:15pm → Second Half Leave.
  • IN from 1:45pm and OUT from 5:15pm → First Half Leave.
  • IN by 9:30am and OUT from 5:15pm → Present.
  • Other combinations require manual attendance selection.

10. Daily Register and Admin Editing

  • Gateman enters timings from physical register.
  • Previous entries are not displayed to Gateman.
  • Admin can review and edit daily records.
  • Admin uses one Save Changes button to save changes across dates and people.
  • Blank IN/OUT clears an existing daily timing record for that employee/date.
  • Non-split employees cannot save an OUT time earlier than IN.
  • Date and employee identify each daily record.
  • UT, SL and OT fields can be manually adjusted for exceptional situations. Manual values are saved as overrides.
  • Person Register changes remain as drafts until Save Changes is pressed.

11. Offline / Mobile Sync

  • Gateman app can save entries locally when internet is unavailable.
  • Pending entries are automatically uploaded when internet becomes available.
  • The app retries pending uploads automatically.
  • A duplicate employee/date conflict is not silently discarded; it remains pending for review.
  • Do not clear browser data or app storage while entries are pending.
  • Synced means pending local entries have been uploaded successfully.

12. Monthly Attendance Report

  • Monthly Report is for attendance only.
  • It shows all active employees.
  • Columns: Present, Half Day, Leave, Absent, Sunday, Holiday.
  • It does not show Worked Hours or OT.
  • For an incomplete current month, attendance is reported only up to latest date for which attendance/record data has been entered.
  • Future dates are not treated as Absent.
  • There is no overall total of all employees; totals are person-wise where applicable.

13. OT / UT Report

  • OT Report is separate from Monthly Attendance Report.
  • It is restricted to OT-eligible employees: Driver and Gateman.
  • It shows every calendar date in selected month.
  • Each employee has Date, IN, UT, OUT, SL, OT and Attendance.
  • IN 2 and OUT 2 are used internally for split-shift employees such as Varinder Pal.
  • Sunday/holiday OT is full elapsed duty time with no break deduction.
  • Regular Driver/Gateman OT uses 5:45pm as normal OUT, with up to 15 minutes ignored: 6:00pm = 0 OT and 6:01pm = 16 minutes OT.
  • Blank dates remain blank.
  • Each employee has a separate subtotal for OT, UT and SL. There is no combined total of all people.
  • Employee selection can include or exclude individual eligible employees.
  • Printed report is arranged one employee per page.
  • Excel export creates one worksheet/tab per employee, named after employee.

14. Important Data Rule

  • Attendance status and timing records are separate.
  • A timing record without an explicitly entered attendance status is treated as Present for monthly attendance reporting, unless date is a Sunday or entered holiday.

15. Admin Areas

  • Attendance: Person Register is used to review and edit daily timings and attendance. Monthly Summary gives month-wise attendance totals.
  • OT Report: detailed OT, UT and SL report.
  • Employees: maintain employee list and employee-specific rules.
  • Holidays: maintain holiday dates.
  • Settings: maintain category rules.

16. Employee-Specific Rules Currently Configured

  • Gautam: Gateman; 525-minute normal target; 7:30am official duty start for UT/SL handling.
  • Varinder Pal: Gateman; 480-minute normal duty; 30-minute rounding; split shift.
  • Barkha: Gateman; 495-minute normal duty; 15-minute break.
  • Umesh: Gardener; 520-minute normal duty; 40-minute break.
  • Staff: 525-minute normal duty; 45-minute break.
  • Driver: 525-minute normal duty; 45-minute break.

Reference rule: If any attendance, UT, SL or OT calculation appears unusual, first check this reference and then check the employee/category rule in Admin Settings.
